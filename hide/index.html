HOW TO PLAY

  • Dark house. Your flashlight is a cone. Aim with the mouse, or drag the right side of the screen.
  • Move with WASD, arrows, or the stick. Sprint makes noise. Charles hears it and comes looking.
  • Hide in closets and under beds. Hold BREATH while he checks or he finds you.
  • USE opens doors, grabs items, takes stairs, and leaves through the front door.
  • Slam is USE on a nearby open door... the LOCK button seals one. NOISE tosses a beeper that lures him.
  • Batteries feed the light. Five doll eyes are optional collectibles. Three voodoo charms open the exit.
  • Win by lasting until dawn, or by carrying 3 charms out the front door. Easy, Normal, or Hard. Two houses.
  • Jump scares are off until you turn them on. The catch screen still shows him either way.

Charles talks crude and loyal, the way he talks to a friend. He is hunting you tonight anyway.
